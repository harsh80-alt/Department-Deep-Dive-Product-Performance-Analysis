# Department Deep Dive: Product Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes product and department performance to understand revenue concentration, category growth, customer segments, regional variation, store formats, seasonality, brands, and customer engagement.

## Business Problem

The objective was to identify high-performing and underperforming parts of the product portfolio, understand customer and regional differences, and identify areas relevant to portfolio, merchandising, promotion, and customer retention analysis.

## Tools & Technologies

- Databricks
- SQL
- Power BI

## Key Work Performed

- Analyzed revenue concentration across departments.
- Reviewed category-level growth and decline.
- Segmented customers into Gold, Silver, Bronze, and non-loyalty groups.
- Analyzed regional product variation.
- Compared Hypermarket and Supermarket shopping patterns.
- Reviewed seasonal patterns and peak and lean periods.
- Analyzed brand leaders, challengers, and private-label opportunities.
- Reviewed customer engagement, repeat behavior, and loyalty migration.

## Key Insights

- The top five departments drove approximately 60–70% of total business.
- Growth categories showed approximately 30–50%+ YoY growth.
- Repeat customers were identified as 3–5x more valuable than one-time customers.
- Festival periods in October–November showed approximately 30–40% uplift.
- Brand leaders held approximately 40–60% share in the analyzed categories.
- Repeat rate across the analyzed engagement view ranged from 30–90%.
- Hypermarket trips were associated with approximately 12–15 items and INR 1,200–1,500 baskets.
- Supermarket trips were associated with approximately 5–8 items and INR 400–600 baskets.

## Business Impact

The analysis provides a framework for balancing traffic-driving and margin-generating categories, tailoring assortment by region and store format, using seasonality for planning, and strengthening customer engagement and retention.
